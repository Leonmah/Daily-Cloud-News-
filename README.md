A daily strategic briefing focused on enterprise AI, AI agents, Forward Deployed Engineering (FDE), China tech/AI, Singapore & Southeast Asia, and cloud/hyperscalers.

## Publishing schedule

Published **daily in the Singapore morning (SGT)**.

## Repository structure

- `news/YYYY-MM-DD.md` — one briefing per day
- Each briefing contains **exactly 5** strategically important developments.

## Editorial priorities

1. Enterprise AI, AI agents, and Forward Deployed Engineering (FDE)
2. China technology and AI
3. Singapore and Southeast Asia business, technology, regulation, and policy
4. Cloud and hyperscalers

Geographic priority: **Singapore / Southeast Asia → China → United States**.

## Briefing format

Each item includes:

- concise headline
- relevant news image when a stable source is available
- 2–4 sentence factual summary
- why it matters strategically
- business / competitive implication
- actionable implication for enterprise cloud / AI delivery and services
- source links

Facts are kept separate from interpretation. Primary sources and high-quality reporting are preferred, duplicate stories are avoided, and developments from the previous 24 hours are prioritized.
